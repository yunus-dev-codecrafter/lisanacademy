

Quran Memorization & Muraja'ah Feature — Lisanun Mubeen Academy

I want to add a Qur'an Memorization Planner/Progress System to the existing Lisanun Mubeen Academy website.

The attached PDF contains the memorization methodology that the feature should be based on.

Important: Before writing code, inspect the existing Lisanun Mubeen Academy codebase and understand its current authentication, student accounts, database structure, admin/student dashboards, existing UI components, and coding conventions. Do not unnecessarily rebuild existing functionality.

The new feature should integrate naturally into the existing system.

 

​Core concept

The system is based on a student memorizing the Qur'an one page at a time.

The default progression is:

Memorize → revise the recently memorized pages → continue memorizing → periodic larger Muraja'ah → continue until the entire Qur'an is memorized.

The PDF uses a standard 604-page Qur'an structure.

The ultimate target is:

Page 1 → Page 604

The PDF's final summary indicates:

604 memorization days

174 revision days

2 days allocated to prayer/thankfulness/celebration

780 total days

The PDF then recommends continuing to review the entire Qur'an regularly after completion.

 

​Basic daily memorization rule

The default pace is:

1 page per memorization day.

For example:

Day 1 → Memorize Page 1
Day 2 → Memorize Page 2
Day 3 → Memorize Page 3
Day 4 → Memorize Page 4
Day 5 → Memorize Page 5

Day 13 → Memorize Page 12
Day 14 → Muraja'ah Pages 7–12

The same pattern continues.

So every six newly memorized pages are followed by a revision day covering those six pages.

This is one of the most important parts of the system.

​Every 100 pages

Four 25-page blocks make approximately:

25 × 4 = 100 pages

After completing a 100-page section, the PDF introduces a larger seven-day Muraja'ah cycle.

The purpose is to revise the entire 100 newly memorized pages rather than treating them only as individual 25-page blocks.

The seven-day revision is distributed progressively.

​Special handling of the first 304 pages

The PDF treats the first half of the Qur'an specially.

The first section runs through Page 304, ending at the end of Surah Al-Kahf.

The PDF explicitly says that at this point the student has completed half of the Qur'an:

From the beginning of Surah Al-Fatihah to the end of Surah Al-Kahf.

It then instructs the student to review all of it and recommends presenting the memorization to a proficient teacher, preferably once at the end of each month.

Therefore, after Page 304:

Major milestone:
FIRST HALF OF QUR'AN COMPLETED

Then initiate a major 304-page Muraja'ah cycle.

The PDF distributes this revision over seven days.
